How It Works

  1. You will receive your own unique Zaylora invite link.
  2. Share the invite link on Instagram, TikTok, WhatsApp, or other social media.
  3. Customers must place their order through your invite link.
  4. You earn 500 Z Points = 5 AED when a referred customer buys 3 or more products in one order.
  5. Orders with only 1 or 2 products do not earn commission.
  6. Points are added only after the customer successfully receives and completes the order.
  7. Cancelled, returned, refunded, fake, or rejected orders will not earn Z Points.
  8. Zaylora may review suspicious or fraudulent referrals before approving points.
  9. Your invite link is personal and should not be misused or used to create fake orders.
  10. Payouts are based on your approved Z Points balance and Zaylora’s payout rules.
